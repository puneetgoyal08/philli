# Philippines 10-Day Trip Planner

## 24 December 2026 -- 2 January 2027

**Starting point:** Bengaluru, India\
**Trip length:** 10 calendar days / 9 nights\
**Travel period:** Christmas + New Year peak season\
**Purpose:** Compare practical Philippines itineraries, understand what
each experience is actually like, and choose a route based on personal
priorities.

> **Planning note:** Flight, hotel, ferry, and tour availability/prices
> can change substantially during Christmas and New Year. Treat all
> price examples in this document as search-time indications and
> re-check live availability before booking.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 1. Executive Summary

There are three strong ways to structure a 10-day Philippines holiday
around Christmas and New Year:

### Option A --- El Nido + Coron

**Route:** Bengaluru → Manila → El Nido → Coron → Manila → Bengaluru

The most concentrated Palawan experience. It combines limestone lagoons,
beaches, island-hopping, snorkeling/diving, and a quieter island-town
atmosphere.

**Trip character:** Scenic + adventurous + island focused\
**Transfer load:** Medium\
**Best for:** Travelers who want classic Palawan scenery and don't mind
moving between two island destinations.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

### Option B --- Boracay + El Nido

**Route:** Bengaluru → Manila → Boracay → El Nido → Manila → Bengaluru

Combines Boracay's famous White Beach, sunset, restaurants and nightlife
with El Nido's lagoons and island-hopping.

**Trip character:** Beach + nightlife + scenic island experiences\
**Transfer load:** Medium/high\
**Best for:** Travelers who want a mix of beautiful beaches, social
atmosphere, restaurants and island-hopping.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

### Option C --- Cebu/Bohol + El Nido

**Route:** Bengaluru → Manila → Cebu/Bohol → El Nido → Manila →
Bengaluru

Adds more variety: waterfalls, countryside, marine activities, cultural
experiences and Palawan scenery.

**Trip character:** Variety + adventure + culture + beaches\
**Transfer load:** High\
**Best for:** Travelers who want to see more of the Philippines and are
comfortable with a faster pace.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 2. What This Trip Should Optimize

Because the trip is only 10 days and includes Christmas and New Year,
the goal should not be to visit the maximum number of islands.

The itinerary should optimize:

1.  **Experience density** --- memorable experiences without spending
    the holiday in transit.
2.  **Transfer efficiency** --- fewer airport/ferry changes.
3.  **Weather resilience** --- enough buffer for rough seas or delays.
4.  **Beach/island quality** --- maximize time in the places that make
    the Philippines distinctive.
5.  **New Year's experience** --- choose the NYE location intentionally.
6.  **Recovery time** --- avoid scheduling a demanding activity
    immediately after a long transfer.
7.  **Booking practicality** --- secure the high-risk/high-demand
    components first.
8.  **Budget control** --- understand where peak-season premiums are
    likely.

# 4. OPTION A --- EL NIDO + CORON

## Route

**Bengaluru → Manila → El Nido → Coron → Manila → Bengaluru**

### Suggested allocation

-   Manila: 1--2 nights
-   El Nido: 3 nights
-   Coron: 4 nights
-   Travel/buffer: integrated into transfer days

### Why this route works

El Nido and Coron complement each other.

**El Nido** - Limestone cliffs - Big and small lagoons - Kayaking -
White-sand beaches - Island-hopping - Sunset experiences

**Coron** - Kayangan Lake - Twin Lagoon - Barracuda Lake - Snorkeling -
Coral gardens - Japanese shipwrecks for divers/snorkelers where
appropriate - Maquinit Hot Spring

The two destinations are different enough that the trip does not feel
like repeating the same experience.

# 5. OPTION A --- DAY-BY-DAY PLAN

**Caution.** You do not need to swim to do this trip. Tell every boat operator before paying: “I don’t swim. No jumps. Life jacket on in the water.” Skip anything sold as a jump, cliff entry, or swim-through.

**Overview.** Land at NAIA, hotel transfer, sleep

### Plan

-   Fly Bengaluru to Manila.
-   Check into a hotel close to the area you want to explore.

-   Sleep early if the flight was long.

-   Keep some Philippine pesos available.

# 6. EL NIDO ACTIVITY GUIDE

## Activity 1 --- Island-Hopping Tour A

**Overview.** Big Lagoon, Secret Lagoon — confirm, Shimizu, Payong-Payong, Seven Commandos

### What you are getting into

A classic El Nido day on a shared or private boat, typically involving
lagoons, beaches, snorkeling and scenic limestone formations.

### Typical experience

-   Boat ride
-   Swimming
-   Snorkeling
-   Lagoon/kayak sections
-   Beach stops
-   Lunch on/near an island

### Physical intensity

**Low to moderate**

Swimming is not required. Some stops involve getting in and out of a
boat repeatedly. See the lagoons from the boat and the beach.

### Caution

Lagoons can be seen from the boat and beach. Secret Lagoon’s usual way in is a short swim through a gap. Ask the operator to skip that stop, or to take you only with a life jacket and a guide if there is a ladder. Do not count on climbing in unaided. No cliff jumps.

### Take

-   Dry bag
-   Waterproof phone pouch
-   Reef-conscious sunscreen
-   Sunglasses
-   Hat
-   Water shoes
-   Quick-dry clothes
-   Small towel
-   Cash

### Watch out for

-   Crowds at famous lagoons
-   Rough seas
-   Sun exposure
-   Wet electronics

### Practical tip

If budget permits, compare a private boat or smaller-group option
against a large shared tour.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Activity 2 --- Island-Hopping Tour C

**Overview.** Hidden Beach, Secret Beach, Matinloc Shrine, Talisay Beach, Helicopter Island, or a rest day

### What you are getting into

A second full-day boat experience with different beaches, lagoons and
snorkeling locations.

### Why do it after Tour A?

The scenery and stops are different enough to justify a second day, but
the activities can still feel repetitive if you do not enjoy boat days.

### Best for

-   Photography
-   Swimming
-   Snorkeling
-   Beaches
-   Scenic landscapes

### Avoid

Trying to combine a full island-hopping day with a long evening
transfer.

### Caution

Beaches and boat landings only. No cliff jumps. The town-day alternative stays the right choice if the previous boat day was enough.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Activity 3 --- El Nido Sunset

A low-effort activity that works well after a demanding tour day.

### Do

-   Find a beachside restaurant/bar.
-   Watch sunset.
-   Have a relaxed dinner.

### Don't

Over-plan the evening. Island-hopping can be tiring.

# 8. CORON ACTIVITY GUIDE

**Overview.** Kayangan viewpoint, Twin Lagoon with a guide, skip Barracuda

## Activity 1 --- Kayangan Lake

### What it is

A famous freshwater lake reached by boat and a climb/stair section.

### Experience

-   Boat trip
-   Short hike/stairs
-   Viewpoint
-   Optional lake swim
-   Limestone landscape

### Caution

Kayangan viewpoint and stairs do not require swimming; the lake swim is optional. Skip Barracuda Lake (it is a swim into deeper water). Twin Lagoon only if the guide stays with you and the jacket stays on.

### Physical intensity

**Moderate**

### Take

-   Water shoes
-   Dry bag
-   Swimwear
-   Water
-   Sun protection

### Avoid

-   Carrying unnecessary luggage.
-   Rushing the climb.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Activity 2 --- Twin Lagoon

### What it is

A dramatic lagoon experience where boats enter between limestone
formations and swimmers experience changing water conditions.

### Experience

-   Boat ride
-   Swimming
-   Limestone cliffs
-   Warm/cool water contrast depending on conditions

### Physical intensity

**Low to moderate**

### Watch out for

-   Slippery surfaces
-   Boat traffic
-   Sea conditions

### Caution

Twin Lagoon only if the guide stays with you and the jacket stays on.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Activity 3 --- Barracuda Lake

Another scenic freshwater/thermal lake experience.

### Best for

-   Swimming
-   Photography
-   Geology/landscape

### Practical point

Combine with other Coron attractions rather than spending a separate day
on it.

### Caution

Skip Barracuda Lake. It is a swim into deeper water.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Activity 4 --- Snorkeling / Wrecks

Coron is particularly interesting for underwater experiences.

### Options

-   Coral gardens
-   Reef snorkeling
-   Shipwreck sites
-   Diving

### Diving note

If diving, do not schedule flights too soon after your final dive.
Follow appropriate diving safety intervals.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Activity 5 --- Maquinit Hot Spring

### Best timing

Late afternoon/evening.

### Why

It is a low-effort activity after a day on the water.

### Avoid

Going immediately after intense sun exposure without rehydrating.

### Caution

Shallow hot-spring pools; sit and wade. Not a swimming test. Still no head-under expectation.

## Watch out

Official pages only. Re-read in this file from the 25 September 2026
check. Do not treat a blog’s customs sentence as the rule.

**India, on arrival.** [Baggage Rules, 2026](https://www.indiabudget.gov.in/doc/cen/cus1426.pdf)
and the [declaration regulations](https://www.indiabudget.gov.in/doc/cen/cus1526.pdf):
accompanied articles other than Annexure I up to ₹75,000; travel souvenirs
duty-free apart from that list; one laptop duty-free from age 18; alcohol
above 2 litres, and the stated tobacco limits, sit outside that clearance;
the jewellery weight allowance applies only after more than one year
abroad, so it does not apply to this 10-day trip; declare fruit and
animal products and use the Red Channel when the form says yes; wildlife
products are listed as prohibited imports.

**Leaving the Philippines.** [Bangko Sentral](https://www.bsp.gov.ph/SitePages/Regulations/GuideToFx.aspx?TabId=1):
₱50,000 in pesos without prior authorisation; foreign currency up to
US$10,000 without a written declaration.

**Coral.** [Fisheries Administrative Order No. 202 (2000)](https://elibrary.judiciary.gov.ph/thebookshelf/showdocs/10/40555),
also listed by [BFAR](https://www.bfar.da.gov.ph/laws-regulations-issuances/administrative-orders/):
do not buy coral.

**Shells.** Leave them out. A BFAR order on shelled mollusks was not fully
read.

**NAIA.** [Shop directory](https://www.newnaia.com.ph/discover/shops),
page updated 25 August 2026: Duty Free is listed at Terminals 1, 2, and 3,
and the page does not say what it sells. A liquor shop is listed at
Terminal 1 pre-departure. Pasalubong counters are listed in Terminals 1
and 3. Hours for 2 January 2027 are not stated. A duty-free shop at El
Nido or Busuanga was not on that page. **Unverified.**

## Suggestion

On Option A, buy a small non-liquid gift in El Nido or Coron only if that
shop is open. Leave alcohol for a NAIA pre-departure counter on the
ticketed terminal, inside the Indian limit above. Pack before the
Busuanga flight. A tight Manila connection is a poor time to hunt for a

## Day 2 --- 25 Dec --- Manila → Caticlan → Boracay

**Overview.** Fly to Caticlan, boat to White Beach, sunset on the sand

### Plan

Wading only. Stay waist-deep.

# 18. WEATHER + SEA CONDITIONS

## Verified: is this a good time?

**Yes.** Your dates, 24 December 2026–2 January 2027, fall inside
PAGASA's **cool dry season** (December–February). That makes them a good
fit for beaches, lagoons, lakes, and outdoor days. It does not guarantee
clear skies or calm seas.
[PAGASA climate overview](https://www.pagasa.dost.gov.ph/information/climate-philippines),
accessed 26 September 2026; checked the season definition and national
climate description.

## Suggestion: the best time of year

For this route, **December to February is the best overall window**: a
good balance of warmth and the drier season. **January and February are the
best months if you can choose freely**. March to May is also in the dry
season, but it is hotter. June to November is the rainy season and is less
reliable for a ferry-heavy island trip. This is a planning recommendation
based on PAGASA's season definitions, not a day-by-day forecast.

The exact weather for 24 December 2026–2 January 2027 is **unverified** at
this planning stage. Check PAGASA's [tourist-area forecast](https://bagong.pagasa.dost.gov.ph/weather/weather-outlook-selected-tourist-areas),
[marine forecast](https://bagong.pagasa.dost.gov.ph/marine/high-seas-forecast),
and [tropical-cyclone advisories](https://bagong.pagasa.dost.gov.ph/tropical-cyclone-advisory-iframe)
shortly before travel. These live products were checked 26 September 2026.

# 32. WHAT NOT TO DO IN THE PHILIPPINES

## 1. Don't over-pack the itinerary

Island travel is slower than a city itinerary.

## 2. Don't underestimate boat travel

A beautiful island day can still be tiring.

## 3. Don't assume every transfer is guaranteed

## 6. Don't assume the cheapest tour is the best value

Number of stops - Start time
